<p><strong>Synopsis</strong></p>
<p>This extraordinary and unique book contains a detailed survey of 500 varieties of pears, accompanied by beautfiul botanic colour illustrations. The first book in this series, <em>The Book of Apples</em>, received many accolades and was quickly recognised as a classic (later updated as <em>The New Book of Apples</em>). <em>The Book of Pears</em> includes full historical, geographical and botanical details as well as exploring the role of pears in cooking, gardening and history. With a full directory, index and comprehensive notes, this is an indespensable and one-of-a-kind reference guide.</p>
