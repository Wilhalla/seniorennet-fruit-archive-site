<p><strong>Feedback Adrian
Baggaley</strong></p>
<p>Hello Daniel, it was nice to see you all last month,
you had nice weather for the UK tour.</p>

<p>Peter Glazebrook won many prizes in the Giant
vegetable Competition at the Harrogate Show, he had the heaviest single onion
which weighed in at fifteen pounds and one and a half ounces (6.86 kg ), he
also won the three heaviest onions, the heaviest cabbage (30.5kg ), heaviest
potato and the longest bean. he came second in the largest marrow.</p>

<p>I Have been summer pruning the pears today and digging
my main crop potatoes, most of my peaches are now lying on the polytunnel
floor, I don't have the time to pick them, most will get used though.</p>

<p>I enclose a picture of Peter's prize winning onion and
cabbage. Also going back to <u>July </u>a picture of my basket at <u>Tatton
Park Fruit &amp; Vegetable Competition, </u>which you might find interesting,
best wishes Adrian.</p>
