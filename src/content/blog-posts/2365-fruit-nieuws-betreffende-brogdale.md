<p><b>Nieuws betreffende Brogdale</b></p>

<p><i>Ik
ontvang net volgende mail van mijn Engelse Fruitvriend Howard Stringer. </i><i>(Zie blog van 24 sept.)</i></p>

<p>Hallo Daniel,</p>

<p>You will be happy to know that the National Fruit Collections will
remain at Brogdale.</p>

<p>The Brogdale Trust has lost the new contract and they will now be
managed by Reading University, who will be keeping the Collection where it is
now.</p>

<p>So you can book for a visit again there. I will tell you how to do this
when I know. It was only announced yesterday (20/12), so neither the
Trust, nor the new set-up have made any detailed plans. The new contract will
only come into force at the end of March.</p>

<p>For a copy of the Defra announcement see Joan's blog, "Fruit
Forum"</p>

<p>Spread this around your circle please. - Howard.</p>
