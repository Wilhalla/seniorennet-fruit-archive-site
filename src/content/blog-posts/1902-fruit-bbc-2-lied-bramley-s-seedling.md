<p>BBC 2 <strong>Lied 
Bramley’s Seedling</strong></p>

<p>Onze groep was figurant voor
de opname in de regen van het <strong>lied voor en
bij de 200 jarige Bramley’s Seedling</strong> met de schrijver van het lied, een
locale Bramleykweker.</p>
<p><strong>Uitzending</strong></p>
<p><u>Morgen</u><u>
woensdag</u><u>, 2 september</u>: BBC 2, om 13u30 onze tijd in
het programma Working Lunch</p>
<p><strong>Lied Bramley’s Seedling</strong></p>
<p>Bi -Centenary Song of the Bramley Apple.</p>
<p>Near the Minster in Southwell, how it happened’s a puzzle,</p>
<p>Little Mary Ann created a
new variety</p>
<p>In the shade of her garden after frost made it harden,</p>
<p>She planted a pip from a
friend’s apple tree.</p>
<p>Oh, sing of the Apple, oh sing of the Apple, what sort are you going
to be?</p>
<p>Oh, sing of the Apple, oh sing of the Apple, what is to become of
thee?</p>
<p>Some forthy years later, Matthew Bramley proprietor,</p>
<p>He finds in his garden Mary Ann’s grown - up tree,</p>
<p>By now one could savour the fruits full of flavour,</p>
<p>Matthew handed them out round the city for free.</p>
<p>Oh, sing of the Apple, oh sing of the Apple, you’re now a celebrity!</p>
<p>Oh, sing of the Apple, oh sing of the Apple, a future’s assured for
thee !</p>
<p>Merryweather’s ambition,
with Matthew’s permission,</p>
<p>Was to multiply maidens from the seedling well-tried,</p>
<p>With crab root stock mixture, hot wax for a fixture</p>
<p>He crafted new fruit trees
and new orchards supplied!</p>
<p>Oh, sing of the Bramley, oh sing of the Bramley, in plantations far
and wide</p>
<p>Oh, sing of the Bramley, oh sing of the Bramley, in thee we take such
pride!</p>
<p>Two hundred years later, no fame could be greater</p>
<p>Than the flavour of those apples from Mary Ann’s tree,</p>
<p>In pies they’re eponymous, in kitchens synonymous</p>
<p>With the culinary heights of our great history</p>
<p>So sing of the Bramley, oh sing of the Bramley, as loud as can be,</p>
<p>Oh sing of the Bramley, oh sing of the Bramley, we are proud to honour
thee!</p>
<p>Tekst John Starkey, lokaal Bramley teler</p>
<p>Muziek
Simon Freeman</p>
