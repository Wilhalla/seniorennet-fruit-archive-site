 <h3>October
 15-16, 2011<br>
 11am-4pm<br>
 <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156534/1405708/goto:http:/www.botanicalgarden.ubc.ca/apple-festival">botanicalgarden.ubc.ca/apple-festival</a></h3>
 
 <p><strong>Apple
 Festival updates and reminders: </strong></p>
 <ul>
 <li>Admission is $4 and is cash only. To keep the
 line moving quickly exact change is appreciated</li>
 <li>The truck of apples arrived early this morning
 and we've now added the list of apples available to buy and taste,
 as well as the list of apple trees for purchase to our <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156535/1405708/goto:http:/www.botanicalgarden.ubc.ca/apple-festival">website.</a> This year we
 have over 41,000 pounds of delicious apples and 25% of them are
 organic!</li>
 <li>We have a bike valet service at our main
 entrance. Biking and walking are your fastest, easiest and cheapest
 way to access the event. If you do drive, there is a FREE shuttle
 bus from the UBC Campus West Parkade</li>
 <li>No pets are allowed in the botanical garden.
 Please leave your furry friends at home</li>
 <li><a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156536/1405708/goto:http:/bcfta.ca/">BC Fruit Testers Association</a> will be hosting a
 physical display of the nearly 200 apple varieties that are still
 grown in British Columbia. Members of the association are also
 on-hand to give demonstrations of grafting and cider-pressing. Try
 to stump them with apple varieties from your garden, as they are
 experts at identifying apples (hint: bring along six apples from
 your unidentified tree)</li>
 </ul>
 
 <p><strong>We are
 also pleased that our friends at the UBC Farm will be joining the fun
 with Heritage Orchard Tours. See below for more information:</strong></p>
 <p><br>
 On October 15 and 16, 2011 the
 UBC Farm is excited to be joining the UBC Botanical Garden Apple
 Festival. In addition to a booth on-site at the festival both days, we
 will be offering tours of the UBC Farm Heritage Orchard on Saturday
 October 15 at 10am, noon and 2pm.<br>
 <br>
 The UBC Farm Heritage Orchard
 was designed, grafted and planted as a student directed study beginning
 in 2005. A sanctuary for apple culture, the orchard is composed of ~120
 trees, including ~70 varieties of apples and a handful of plums. 2011
 marks our first year of significant harvest for market sales and the
 orchard is producing gorgeous apples. Come visit this urban agricultural
 gem for tours with <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156537/1405708/goto:http:/www.landfood.ubc.ca/ubcfarm/">UBC
 Farm</a> staff who care for the orchard on SATURDAY October 15 at the UBC
 Farm! Meet at the UBC Farm entrance gate. No UBC Farm orchard tours will
 be offered on Sunday.<br>
 <br>
 The UBC Farm is a 20-30 minute
 walk, a 10 minute bike ride and a 5 minute drive from the Botanical
 Garden. See <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156538/1405708/goto:http:/maps.google.com/maps/ms?ie=UTF8&amp;hl=en&amp;msa=0&amp;msid=105676020697289971829.0004831f51e0d26b65f55&amp;t=h&amp;z=15">map
 link</a> for details. Tours of the UBC Farm Heritage Orchard are free,
 though donations are gratefully accepted.<br>
 <br>
 For more about the UBC Farm
 Orchard, check out this <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156539/1405708/goto:http:/friendsoftheubcfarm.wordpress.com/2009/10/13/spartans-and-pippins-and-winesaps-oh-my/">post
 on the Friends of the UBC Farm blog</a>. Also, be sure to follow the UBC
 Farm on <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156540/1405708/goto:https:/www.facebook.com/pages/Centre-for-Sustainable-Food-Systems-at-UBC-Farm/124231794284990">Facebook</a>
 and <a href="http://e2ma.net/go/7300837337/208703678/226156541/1405708/goto:http:/twitter.com/%23%21/ubcfarm">Twitte</a>r
 for info about its research, learning, and quirky site happenings.</p>
